NORDKAPP ENGINEERING AS - HSE INCIDENT REPORT
Report no: HSE-2026-0117
Classification: Near miss (high potential)
Location: Fjordvik site, Module 3 scaffold, level +12 m

Description
At 10:40 a 0.6 kg torque wrench fell from the scaffold platform at level +12 m and landed inside the barriered zone below. The zone was correctly barriered and nobody was inside it. The wrench was not secured with a tool lanyard.

Immediate actions
- Work at height on the scaffold was stopped for 30 minutes.
- All tools at height were checked for lanyards.
- Toolbox talk held with the mechanical crew.

Root cause
- Tool lanyards were available but not used because the crew considered the job "short duration".
- Toe board on one scaffold bay was missing after a modification the day before.

Corrective actions
1. Scaffold inspection after every modification, signed by the scaffold supervisor. Owner: Scaffold lead. Due: week 43.
2. Dropped objects campaign for all crews on site. Owner: HSE advisor. Due: week 44.
3. Spot checks of tool lanyards twice per week. Owner: Area supervisors. Due: ongoing.

Potential severity: Serious injury. Actual severity: None.
